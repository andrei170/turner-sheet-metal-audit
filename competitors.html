Tatro in Garden City publishes the job, the benefits and an application form. Nobody else does, including your neighbour in Scott City.

That is unusual, and it is good news. In most trades the recruitment lane is crowded. Here it is almost empty — but the one firm using it is also the biggest employer nearby.

What Tatro Does That Works
The one to learn from
  • A page that sells the job, not just lists it — benefits written out, not "competitive package".
  • An application form on the page, so somebody can apply from a phone on their lunch break.
  • A registered apprenticeship, which means they are growing their own technicians rather than only competing for finished ones.
  • Founded 1952 and they say so. You have 1977 and say nothing.
And what that means for you

Tatro is 45 miles away and is the firm most likely to take the technician you want. Everyone else in the area is invisible to a job seeker — which is exactly the gap a proper careers page fills, without you having to outbid anybody.

Where You Can Win
A small firm has things a big one cannot offer
  • Nearly fifty years trading. For a technician with a family, that is security.
  • Bryant Factory Authorized Dealer — proper equipment and manufacturer training.
  • You are the owners. They work with Ron and Pam, not a regional manager.
  • Scott City itself. Someone local keeps a short commute instead of driving to Garden City.
  • Only two hires needed. You are not trying to fill a rota of twenty. Two good people changes the year.
What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Whether any of these firms advertise jobs elsewhere — Facebook, word of mouth, colleges. We checked their own websites.
  • Anyone's pay rates, crew size or staff turnover, including yours.
  • Your own Facebook hiring posts — the page is not readable without logging in.
  • Every HVAC firm in western Kansas. This is the set we could find and check.

One firm nearby is asking properly. Be the second.

It is one page, the right questions, and an advert pointed at people already doing the work.
